## Claims = Provenance Required

A single sentence may produce multiple claims if it contains multiple facts. Avoid redundant overlapping claims.

Every claim needs:
- claim_type: date/location/identity/relationship/detail
- subject: what/who (never a pronoun, make specific: prefer "grandfather's arrival" over "arrival")
- claim_value: the information
- claimed_by: sender name OR attributed person (for hearsay, use sender unless specific person named)
- claimed_by_source: direct/attributed/hearsay
- certainty_language: if hedging ("around", "I think")

**Types:**
- date: when something happened. Combine calculated years with resolved dates for original events.
- location: where something happened/someone is from
- identity: "Dexter's ex-wife" → "Judy Dor"
- relationship: how two people relate
- detail: other context (weather, mood, descriptions)

Ex: "grandfather arrived around 1889" → claim_type: "date", subject: "grandfather's arrival", claim_value: "1889", certainty_language: "around"
Ex: "turns 34 in 4 days" → claim_type: "date", subject: "birth date", claim_value: "January 31, 1992" (using birth_year 1992, not 2026)
