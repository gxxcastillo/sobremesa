# Sobremesa — Product Definition

## What Sobremesa Is

Sobremesa is a **conversation-first family memory platform**.

It works by introducing AI facilitators into existing group chats
(e.g. family group messages) to gently encourage, capture, and preserve
family stories as they naturally emerge.

Sobremesa does **not** replace family conversations.
It listens, responds, and nudges at appropriate moments to help stories
be remembered instead of lost.

---

## Core Purpose

Families already tell their stories — casually, emotionally, and imperfectly —
in chat threads that are ephemeral and unstructured.

Sobremesa exists to:
- Surface meaningful stories at the right time
- Encourage deeper recollection without interrogation
- Preserve what was said *as it was said*
- Organize memories **without disrupting conversation**

The primary goal is **long-term preservation with warmth**, not efficiency,
accuracy, or completeness.

---

## What the Product Does

Sobremesa:
- Participates in group chats as a respectful presence
- Asks thoughtful follow-up questions when appropriate
- Recognizes stories, people, places, and events in conversation
- Preserves multiple perspectives and conflicting memories
- Builds a structured family history **from natural conversation**

---

## What the Product Is NOT

Sobremesa is not:
- A genealogy tool that enforces a single “correct” history
- A chat bot that dominates or steers conversations
- A transcription or surveillance system
- A replacement for human storytelling
- A productivity or engagement-maximization tool

Silence, pauses, and emotional moments are respected.

---

## Target Users

- Families with shared group chats
- Multi-generational, multilingual families
- Families who value memory, culture, and storytelling
- Non-technical users

The product assumes **no training, no dashboards, and no active management**
by family members.

---

## Non-Negotiable Product Principles

- Warmth over efficiency
- Preservation over resolution
- Respect over completeness
- Listening before asking
- Human conversation always comes first

If a trade-off conflicts with these principles, the trade-off is rejected.
